## 🕹️ About

Sunken Realms is a third-person action RPG and dungeon crawler developed and published by Magic Puncher Games. You play as Alric, a son who descends into an abandoned underground city to uncover what happened to his missing father. Explore a hand-crafted, procedurally arranged dungeon, fight monsters, collect materials and loot, and master forgotten spells. Return to the surface to craft weapons and armor, brew potions, prepare food, upgrade stats at the Soul Forge, and challenge deeper regions at higher difficulty levels.

Sunken Realms is for players who enjoy atmospheric fantasy dungeon crawlers, action RPG combat, crafting, exploration, and repeatable high-risk expeditions.

## ✨ Features

- 🔨 **Gear Crafting** — Collect materials and create stronger weapons and armor.
- 🧪 **Camp Preparation** — Brew potions, prepare food, and improve your chances before each descent.
- ⚔️ **Action RPG Combat** — Battle monsters through focused third-person hack-and-slash combat.
- 🏰 **Lost Underground City** — Explore the cursed ruins of Aedaria and uncover its forgotten history.
- 💀 **Rogue-Lite Progression** — Push into higher difficulty levels for greater danger and better loot.
- 🔥 **Soul Forge** — Spend your spoils to upgrade stats and develop your character.
- ✨ **Forgotten Spells** — Master ancient magic and use it against the dangers below.
- 🗺️ **Procedural Dungeon Design** — Navigate hand-crafted dungeon sections arranged for varied expeditions.
